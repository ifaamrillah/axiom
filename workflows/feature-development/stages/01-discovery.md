# Stage 1: Discovery

**Skill:** `product-discovery` · **Required:** yes · **Depends on:** nothing

## Entry

A request for new or substantial capability. Skip to a short problem statement when the request already states the outcome, the users, and the acceptance criteria.

## Do

1. Separate the stated request from the underlying problem and the desired outcome.
2. Elicit one question at a time, each with a recommended answer. Stop asking when the outcome and success signal are clear.
3. Propose 2–3 approaches with trade-offs when the solution shape is open.
4. Record goals, non-goals, actors, assumptions, and constraints in `.axiom/workflows/active/<ID>/` (or in the spec draft).

## Exit

The intended outcome and the success signal are understood and written down. Update the stage status in the workflow state.
