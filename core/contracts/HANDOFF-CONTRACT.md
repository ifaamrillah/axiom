# Agent Handoff Contract

A handoff is a compact, self-contained report from a delegated worker to the orchestrator.

## Required information

- Task identity, owner/role, and status
- Result summary
- Files changed
- Deliverables and locations
- Verification performed and evidence
- Decisions made
- Known limitations
- Blockers
- Recommended next action

## Rules

- Do not paste the entire conversation.
- Distinguish verified facts from assumptions.
- Report failed or skipped checks explicitly.
- Do not edit outside the assigned scope without coordination.
- The orchestrator remains responsible for integration and final quality gates.
