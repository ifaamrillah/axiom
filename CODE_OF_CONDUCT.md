# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Axiom an open, welcoming, and respectful experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and evidence, not personal attributes.
- Accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respect different levels of experience and backgrounds.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with appropriate confidentiality. Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
