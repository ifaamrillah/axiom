# Product Discovery

## Purpose

Understand the problem and desired outcome before prescribing a feature.

## When to use / avoid

Use for new product capabilities or materially ambiguous requests. Avoid adding a discovery phase to a small, explicit maintenance change.

## Inputs and preconditions

User request, known product context, available evidence, and constraints. Do not contact users or external parties unless explicitly authorized.

## Procedure

1. Separate the stated request from the underlying problem and desired outcome.
2. Identify affected users, current behavior, constraints, and measurable success signals.
3. Review existing product docs and code for prior decisions or capabilities.
4. List assumptions and alternative approaches with impact and cost.
5. Ask only for decisions that materially change scope or risk; otherwise document a reversible assumption.

## Outputs and gates

Problem statement, outcomes, constraints, assumptions, and open decisions. Gate: proposed work traces to a real need and success can be assessed.
