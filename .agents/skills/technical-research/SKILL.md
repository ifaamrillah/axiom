# Technical Research

## Procedure

1. Frame the exact question, decision, relevant versions, and constraints.
2. Prefer official documentation, release notes, migration guides, standards, and primary source repositories.
3. Verify that sources match the project's actual versions and environment.
4. Cross-check important or risky claims with a second authoritative source where practical.
5. Distinguish documented behavior, observed behavior, recommendations, and uncertainty.
6. Record source title, URL, access date, applicable version, and key finding.
7. Summarize implications and alternatives; do not dump raw research into the project context.
8. Identify facts that require a local proof-of-concept or test.

## Outputs

A concise research brief with question, context, findings, source links, version scope, alternatives, recommendation, and limitations.

## Quality

Never fabricate citations or versions. Avoid treating an old blog post or generated summary as authoritative when official docs are available.
